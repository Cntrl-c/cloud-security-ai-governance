# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

*Sample document. Fictional parties. Not a real contract.*

Applies to SOW XYZ-ABC-001 and any approved change requests.

## 1. Communication

| Item | Commitment |
|---|---|
| Status update | Weekly written summary to Client sponsor |
| Business-hours inquiry response | Within 1 business day |
| Working hours | Pacific Time; Hong Kong and Brussels contacts reached via scheduled overlap windows |

## 2. Finding Escalation

| Severity | Provider notifies Client | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** (CVSS 9.0–10.0) or active compromise found | Within **4 hours** of confirmation | Phone + email to sponsor |
| High (7.0–8.9) | Within 1 business day | Email |
| Medium / Low | In the next scheduled report | Report |

If testing reveals signs of a **real attacker** already present, Provider stops testing immediately and notifies the Client sponsor.

## 3. Testing Disruption

- If a test causes a service outage, Provider halts that activity and notifies the technical contact within **30 minutes**.
- Provider assists with rollback but is not responsible for restoring systems the Client did not snapshot beforehand.

## 4. Deliverable Quality

- Each finding includes: affected asset, severity, evidence, business impact, and fix.
- False positives are reviewed and removed before reporting.
- Reports are written for two audiences: an executive summary and technical detail.

## 5. Client Commitments

| Item | Commitment |
|---|---|
| Technical contact availability | During all approved testing windows |
| Deliverable review | Within 5 business days |
| Critical finding decision (fix or accept) | Before pen testing begins |

## 6. Missed Commitments

If Provider misses a deliverable date by more than 5 business days for reasons within its control, the Client may request a revised plan and a credit as defined in the master agreement. Delays caused by late Client access or approvals extend the schedule day-for-day.

## 7. Review

SLA performance is reviewed at the executive readout at the end of each phase.
